Detection models  (not bundled; downloaded on first use)
--
The built-in models are not committed to the CloakFrame repository or bundled
with its packages. The application downloads each one from its pinned upstream
location on first use, verifies its SHA-256 digest, and caches it locally. Their
terms are separate from CloakFrame's license; confirm the upstream terms before
commercial use or redistribution.

YOLO5Face-n (recommended face detector)
  ONNX conversion from the yolov5-face-onnx-inference release:
  https://github.com/yakhyo/yolov5-face-onnx-inference/releases/tag/weights
  It is based on YOLO5Face (https://github.com/deepcam-cn/yolov5-face), whose
  code is GPL-3.0, and was trained on the WIDER FACE dataset, which is
  restricted to non-commercial research use. Treat this model as
  non-commercial only.

YuNet (fast face detector)
  face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx from the OpenCV Zoo:
  https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet
  That directory identifies the model license as MIT.

License-plate detector
  From open-image-models (https://github.com/ankandrew/open-image-models),
  which is MIT-licensed. It uses the YOLOv9 architecture and remains subject to
  its upstream project's terms.

Citations
  Qi, Tan, Yao, Liu. YOLO5Face: Why Reinventing a Face Detector.
    arXiv:2105.12931, 2021.
  Wang, Liao. YOLOv9: Learning What You Want to Learn Using Programmable
    Gradient Information. arXiv:2402.13616, 2024.

## Updates

- Accepting an update now installs it for you. Windows and Linux update in
  place, and macOS installs the update instead of opening a download page.
- Later updates download only the part that changed.
- You are still asked first, and the check at startup can be turned off in
  Settings.

## New face models

- YOLO5Face-n is the recommended model for photos and videos.
- YuNet is a lighter choice for slower machines, under 0.3 MB to download.
- You can still bring your own ONNX model through **Browse…**.

## Cover faces with your own image

- Pick a PNG, JPEG, BMP, TIFF, or WebP image to use in place of a mosaic — in
  photos, videos, style previews, and review.
- The image keeps its shape and tilts along with the face.
- In video it follows the face smoothly as it moves.
- Transparent parts never let the original show through.

## webm videos

- You can now drop `.webm` files in alongside the formats already supported.

## Video review

- Draw a box over anything the app missed, before the video is saved.
- Set the stretch of time each box applies to.
- Move a box as the subject moves, and the frames in between are filled in.
- Remove boxes you drew, or keep leaving out ones the app found by mistake.

## You are told what might not be covered

- If anything could have been missed — faces beyond the limit in a crowded
  photo, a track dropped for being uncertain, a gap the app could not fill in —
  the run ends in **Review required** instead of **Done** and tells you how many.
- Video tracks with only a few clear detections are now included by default.
  Leaving one out is your choice to make in review.

## Video

- What you see in review is exactly what gets saved, and the audio lines up
  with it.
- Video keeps its correct proportions, anamorphic footage included.
- Every audio track is kept. Before, only the first survived, so commentary and
  other-language tracks disappeared without a word.
- A damaged video now fails instead of quietly saving a shortened result.
- If hardware encoding fails partway through, the app finishes the job in
  software rather than giving up on the file.
- Mosaics no longer drift across a cut when the shot changes twice within a few
  frames.

## Reliability

- A photo too large for the memory available is skipped with a reason, instead
  of risking the app running out.
- **Stop** pressed right after **Start** is no longer ignored.
- The app no longer quits when the system is short on resources.
- Solid fill is no longer see-through on images with transparency.
- An interrupted save no longer leaves a half-written file under the finished
  name.
- On Windows, photo details are kept even when the path has non-English
  characters.
- Your existing Fast or Accurate choice carries over to the new model list.
- Downloaded models are checked before they are used.
- If a custom ONNX model file changes after you approve it, you are asked again
  before it is loaded.
- If the app is killed or the machine loses power mid-run, the temporary copy it
  made of your original is cleaned up the next time you start it. Until now it
  stayed behind.

## Language

- Video errors and progress messages now appear in Korean, Japanese, and
  Simplified Chinese. Until now they were in English whatever your language.

## Models and licensing

- The built-in models are downloaded the first time you use them, then work
  offline.
- YOLO5Face-n is trained on WIDER FACE data and should be treated as
  non-commercial only.
- YuNet is MIT-licensed, and models you bring yourself keep their own terms.
